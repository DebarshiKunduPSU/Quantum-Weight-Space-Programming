**Design idea.** In 8×8 digits, a 4 has an open top (two separate strokes, little ink at row 0 col 3), a long crossbar reaching far left (row 4–5, col 1) and far right (rows 3–4, col 6), and a bottom that stays at cols 3–4. A 9 has a closed loop with a top bar centred on col 3, no ink at (4,1)/(5,1) or (3,6)/(4,6), and its stem usually curls left at the bottom (rows 6–7, col 2). I build a linear score from these pixels and encode it as a cumulative RY angle on one qubit: θ = π/2 + Σ wᵢxᵢ, so P(|1⟩) = sin²(θ/2) exceeds 0.5 exactly when the weighted sum is positive (class 9). Weights are scaled so θ stays inside (−π/2, 3π/2) where the decision rule is monotone.

**Design idea.** The 9 has a closed loop at the top: ink in the top-middle pixels (row 0, cols 2–3), the top arc crossing row 1 col 3, and a closed loop bottom at row 3 col 3. The 4 instead has an open top (dark gap at col 3 in rows 1–3) and a long horizontal crossbar reaching far left (row 4–5, col 1), which a 9 lacks. I combine these pixels into one linear score and encode it as a rotation angle on a single qubit, offset by π/2 so that the sign of the score decides the measured bit.

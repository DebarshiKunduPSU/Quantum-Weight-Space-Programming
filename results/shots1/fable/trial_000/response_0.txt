**Design idea.** The two digits differ mainly on the *left side of the middle rows*: an 8 has two closed loops, so pixels in columns 1–2 of rows 2–5 carry ink, while a 3 is open on the left and those pixels are dark (its top hook and bottom curl only touch the left edge at rows 1 and 6–7). So I build a single-qubit linear classifier: a bias RY followed by RY rotations whose angles are proportional to the left-middle pixel intensities. The total rotation angle is θ = 0.97 + Σ w·x; P(|1⟩) = sin²(θ/2) exceeds 0.5 exactly when the weighted ink sum passes the threshold (~1.5 in pixel units), and the maximum possible θ stays below 3π/2 so the response is monotone. Qubit 0 measured as 1 → class 8, 0 → class 3.

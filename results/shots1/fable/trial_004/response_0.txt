**Design idea.** The main visual difference between a 3 and an 8 is the left side of the middle rows: an 8 closes both loops on the left (ink in columns 1–2 around rows 3–5), while a 3 is open on the left there (dark). So I accumulate the brightness of pixels x[25], x[26], x[33], x[34], x[41], x[42] (rows 3–5, columns 1–2) into a single RY rotation on one qubit. Consecutive RY rotations add their angles, so the final angle is θ = (π/2 − 0.52) + 0.4·S, where S is the weighted left-side ink. P(|1⟩) = sin²(θ/2) exceeds 0.5 exactly when S exceeds ≈1.3, which separates the dark-left 3 (S ≈ 0.3–1) from the closed-loop 8 (S ≈ 2.5–4). Readout: |0⟩ → class 3, |1⟩ → class 8.

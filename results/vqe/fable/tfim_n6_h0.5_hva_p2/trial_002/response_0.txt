For N=6 the relevant modes are k=π/6, π/2, 5π/6 with weights 1.46, 1.12, 0.62. Two layers cannot steer the k=5π/6 mode fully (it needs p=3), so I optimized the weighted overlap by hand: a first ZZ layer with θ0=π/2 (rotation by π) sends all modes to maximal polar angle, a modest X angle sets the azimuth, then a second ZZ rotation of ~110° and a final X rotation of about −110° bring the dominant modes (k=π/6, π/2) to within a few percent of their targets while giving the hard mode a positive overlap. This estimated ring energy ≈ −5.3 (vs ≈ −4.85 for the best p=1), and the open chain should behave similarly.

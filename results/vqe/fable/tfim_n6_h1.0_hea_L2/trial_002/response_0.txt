**Approach.** The CNOT ladder maps a product state in "domain‑wall" variables d (qubit k after the ladder carries the cumulative parity d₀⊕…⊕d_k) onto the spin basis. In that encoding Z_{k-1}Z_k → Z_k^{(d)} and X_k → X_k^{(d)}X_{k+1}^{(d)} (X₀ → X₀X₁, X₅ → X₅), i.e. the Kramers–Wannier dual TFIM. A product state in d (first ladder acting on |0…0⟩ trivially, so the product state is prepared by the *middle* RY layer, followed by the second ladder, final layer = identity) gives E = −Σcosθ_k − sinθ₁ − sinθ₅ − Σ sinθ_k sinθ_{k+1} with d₀ in |+⟩ (θ=π/2). Self‑consistent optimization gives tanθ₁=1.868, tanθ₂=1.748, tanθ₃=1.736 (symmetric), E ≈ −7.234, clearly better than the spin mean field (−6.8); exact is ≈ −7.30. The first RY layer can only add a first‑order correction through qubit 4 (L|e₄⟩=|000011⟩ overlaps the X₄X₅ dual term): a small θ[4] ≈ 0.09 lowers the energy by ~0.01. The last layer gives no first‑order gain, so it is left at zero.

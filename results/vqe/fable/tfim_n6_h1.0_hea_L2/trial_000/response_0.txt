**Approach.** The CNOT ladder maps a product state in the "domain‑wall" (Kramers–Wannier dual) basis onto a correlated state in the physical basis: under the ladder, Z_iZ_{i+1} → Z_{i+1} and X_i → X_iX_{i+1} (X_5 → X_5). So with the first and last RY layers set to ~0, the circuit prepares U·⊗_j ry(a_j)|0⟩ whose energy is exactly

E = −Σ_{i=1}^{5} cos a_i − Σ_{i=0}^{4} sin a_i sin a_{i+1} − sin a_5 .

This is "dual mean field": a_0 only enters via sin a_0 → a_0 = π/2, and the bulk angles optimize near tan a ≈ 1+sin a (≈61°), giving E ≈ −7.24, clearly below the ordinary product-state mean field (−6.8) and within ~1% of the exact ground energy (≈ −7.3). I solved the stationarity conditions for the symmetric angle pattern (a_1=a_5≈61.9°, a_2=a_4≈60.3°, a_3≈60.1°). Final layer left at zero.

**Approach.** The TFIM maps to free fermions; each momentum mode is a Bloch vector that the ZZ layer rotates (by 4γ) about a k‑dependent axis and the X layer rotates (by 4β) about z. For p=1 this gives the known optimum θ_ZZ=π/8, θ_X=π/4 (energy −1.207/site, vs. −4/π exact). For p=2 I worked in this mode picture for the N=6 modes (k=π/6, π/2, 5π/6): the k=π/2 mode can be kept essentially exact, which fixes layer 1 in terms of layer 2, leaving a 2‑parameter family that I scanned by hand. The best region found is a first layer with a moderate ZZ angle and a large X rotation (≈146°–150° on the Bloch sphere), followed by a larger ZZ angle and a final X rotation of ≈60°. This raises the fidelity of the hard low‑k mode from ~0.58 to ~0.75 while leaving the other modes near 1, improving the estimated energy from −7.24 (p=1) to ≈ −7.43 on the 6‑site ring (exact −7.73).

**Approach.** At h=J the TFIM is free-fermionic; in the pseudo-spin (Bogoliubov mode) picture each momentum mode starts at +z (the |+>ⁿ vacuum), every ZZ layer is a rotation about an axis tilted by k in the z–x plane and every X layer is a rotation about z, and the target ground-state direction of mode k is at polar angle π/2−k/2. A Trotterized adiabatic ramp H(s)=−(1−s)ΣX−sΣZZ from s=0 to s=1/2 gives θ_ZZ,j = 2·dt·s_j, θ_X,j = 2·dt·(1−s_j) with both angles of the same sign (verified by an explicit 2-qubit calculation: E = −sin2θ_X sinθ_ZZ − 2cosθ_ZZ at p=1, whose optimum θ_X=π/4, θ_ZZ≈0.4–0.46 also calibrates the scale dt≈0.5–0.6 per layer).

I tracked the Bloch vectors of representative modes (k=π/4, π/2, 3π/4) by hand for a linear ramp s_j = j/8 with dt = 0.5: all three end within ~11° of their targets (overlaps ≈0.98–0.99), while dt = 0.6 overshoots the high-weight k≈π/2 modes. The small residual out-of-plane component of the heavy modes is reduced slightly by shrinking the final X angle a bit below the Trotter value (0.5 → ~0.47), at a small cost for the low-weight small-k modes. Estimated energy ≈ −7.15 versus the exact −7.30 for the open 6-site chain.

**Design idea.** In these 8x8 digits the clearest difference between 3 and 8 is the left side of the two loops: an 8 has ink on the left edge of both the upper loop (rows 2–3, columns 1–2) and the lower loop (rows 4–5, columns 1–2), while a 3 is open there (those pixels are dark; its ink sits on the right half plus a middle bar). I therefore build a linear score from those left-side pixels and implement it as a single-qubit RY accumulator: all RY angles on one qubit add up, so the final angle is θ = 0.72 + 0.5·(x[17]+x[18]+x[25]+x[33]+x[41]+x[42]), and P(1)=sin²(θ/2) crosses 0.5 when the left-side ink sum is ≈1.7. The bias and weights are chosen so θ stays in roughly [0.7, 3.7], keeping P(1) monotone enough that more left ink always means "8".

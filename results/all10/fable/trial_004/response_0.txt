**Design idea**

With only 3 examples per class I can't fit weights numerically, so I build an *interpretable soft decision tree* from regional ink features that I know separate the digit shapes:

- Root (q0): ink in the lower-left (rows 4–6, cols 1–2) → {0,2,6,8} vs {1,3,4,5,7,9}.
- Within {0,2,6,8} (q1): upper-right ink (col 5–6, rows 0–3) → 6 (dark) vs {0,2,8}; then (q2) centre ink → 0 (dark) vs {2,8}; then (q3) far bottom-right ink → 2 vs 8.
- Within {1,3,4,5,7,9} (q1): left-middle ink (rows 1–4, cols 1–2) → {4,5,9} vs {1,3,7}; then (q2) right ink in rows 2–3 → 5 (dark) vs {4,9}, and top-right ink → 1 (dark) vs {3,7}; then (q3) "row-4 bar reaching col 6 / left" minus "9-like top-left & bottom curl" → 4 vs 9, and lower-right ink → 3 vs 7.

Each decision is a rotation ry(Σ wᵢ xᵢ) on a fresh qubit, so P(1)=sin²(θ/2), with weights scaled so θ stays below ~4.3 (no wrap-around) and the threshold sits at θ=π/2. Conditional branches are implemented with X-flips, ccx into ancillas (q4, q5) and cry gates, then uncomputed. The leaf probability is the product of branch probabilities along the path, i.e. a soft tree; leaves are mapped to classes via bitstring readout on q0–q3.
